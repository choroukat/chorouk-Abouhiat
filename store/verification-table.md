## Official Portals Audit Notes (Phase 4):

1. **`https://www.cnie.ma` (DGSN)**:
   - Timbre fee confirmed: 75 MAD for adult CNIE.
   - Mandatory online appointment booked directly on `cnie.ma`.
   - Dispensation of birth certificate when the citizen has biometric record or family booklet.

2. **`https://www.passeport.ma` (Ministère de l'Intérieur)**:
   - Timbre fee confirmed: 500 MAD adult / 300 MAD minor under 18.
   - Dematerialized stamp purchased via online banking or payment agents (SMS code verification).
   - No prior online appointment required at Al Mouqata'a or Caïdat.

3. **`https://www.minhaty.ma` and `https://www.rsu.ma`**:
   - Compulsory enrollment in RNP (Identifiant IDCS) and RSU before application.
   - Household economic scoring evaluated based on DGI fiscal records and social indicators.

4. **`https://narsa.gov.ma` (NARSA)**:
   - 450 MAD examination rights fee and fiscal stamp.
   - Mandatory medical certificate by accredited medical doctor (-3 months).

5. **`https://www.idarati.ma` (Idarati - Loi 55-19)**:
   - Signature legalization fee: 2 MAD per signature.
   - Strict enforcement of Law 55-19: abolition of duplicate unmandated certified copies.

6. **`https://www.macnss.ma` (CNSS - AMO Tadamon)**:
   - Automatic qualification for households with RSU score below the 9.326 threshold.
   - Free access to public healthcare institutions and AMO coverage.
